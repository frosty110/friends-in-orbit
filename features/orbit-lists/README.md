## Product

### Why it exists

Lists are how users shape their orbit. Not priority tiers, not categories — moods and contexts (inner orbit, late night, people who ground me). Each list has its own rules and active hours. A contact on multiple lists shares one global last-call timestamp — calling them anywhere updates everywhere. Without this cross-list propagation, lists would contradict each other and the app's coherence collapses.

### User story

As a user, I create lists that match how I actually think about my people. Each list surfaces on its own rhythm. When I call someone, every list they're on knows.

### Behavior

**Lists Manager.**
- Rows: name, member count, rhythm summary. A regular list's second line is its rhythm as its interval, whichever rule it runs (LIST-30): "Every 14 days" for Keep in touch, from the per-list override or the template's default, "Every 3 days" for Late night and "Every day" for Energize (their base intervals); a smart list's is its rule ("Recently added · 30 days"). A stored rhythm that cannot be decoded still says "Couldn't read this list's rhythm". Regular lists had no second line until 2026-10-06; Late night and Energize read "Late night rhythm" / "Energize rhythm" until 2026-10-07 (`ListsManagerViewModelTest`).
- One "New list" control (LIST-20).
- Reorder by the row's drag handle, or Move up/down in its menu. Archive removes from home while preserving data. Delete requires confirmation (destructive), then shows "List deleted." with Undo; the delete is held until the snackbar goes away, as on home (2026-10-05).
- **LIST-02: Order and lifecycle are narrow writes.** Lists are ordered by `sortOrder`, and the order is stable: a new list takes `max(sortOrder) + 1` so it lands at the bottom, and a reorder rewrites only `sortOrder`, for the active rows in the moved range, inside the repository's `withTransaction` (`ListRepository.reorder`, `ListDao.updateSortOrder`), with the ViewModel's mutex serialising dispatches. Archive and restore flip `isArchived` and nothing else (`ListRepository.setArchived`, `ListDao.updateArchived`), so memberships and their schedule state survive an archive and come back whole on Restore. Defined 2026-10-06 from what `ListsManagerViewModel`, `ListDao` and `ListRepository` already cited; `ListsManagerViewModelTest` pins archive, restore and the reorder dispatch.
- **LIST-24: An archived list is out of your orbit.** No screen says a person is on one: the contact picker's "On Inner orbit, Late night" line and its "Not on a list" and "On a list" filters, Contact detail's "On these lists", its custom schedule (counted from lists that aren't archived: shown for two or more, or whenever a schedule is saved, CONTACT-03) and the rhythm it follows ("Comes up every 14 days, like the rest of {list}."), and the call log's "from {list}" all leave archived lists out, as Search across everyone always did. The memberships are kept, so Restore brings everything back (LIST-02). One rule decides it (`onActiveLists`, `domain/model/ActiveListMemberships.kt`); the contact picker also keeps its own target list, so Add people on an archived list still hides the people already on it. Until 2026-10-07 the picker said "On Old friends" for an archived list, and someone on an archived list alone was missing from "Not on a list". Pinned by `ActiveListMembershipsTest`, `PickerCandidatesTest`, `ContactPickerViewModelTest`, `ContactDetailViewModelTest` and `CallLogViewModelTest`.
- Per-row overflow actions, in order: rename, **list settings** (opens List Configuration), pause or resume nudges, move up/down, then archive behind the divider (`ListRowMenuOrderTest`). The action that opens List Configuration is labeled **"List settings"** everywhere, not "Configure", matching the home long-press menu (see `features/home/README.md`). Pause nudges / Resume nudges and the Archive line use Home's strings, so the two menus cannot drift word by word; pausing a list's nudges was possible from Home but not from here until 2026-10-06.
- These per-list actions (add people, pause or resume nudges, list settings, archive, delete) are **also reachable via a long-press on the list tile on home**. Home is the convenience surface; Lists Manager remains the full manager. The two must stay consistent: same labels, and the same delete-with-Undo behavior. Spec: `features/home/README.md`, the list tile long-press quick actions section.
- Every confirmation follows the write (rules.md Code 3). "List archived." with Undo, "List restored.", "Nudges paused." / "Nudges on." and List settings' "This is now a regular list." are emitted by the ViewModel only once the write succeeded; a failed write says "Couldn't save your change" and nothing else. Until 2026-10-06 a failed archive queued its success message behind the failure, and the restore and convert messages were the screen's and showed before the write resolved (`ListsManagerViewModelTest`, `ListConfigViewModelTest`).
- Archive, restore and a committed delete ask the widget to refresh (WIDGET-06, `features/widgets/README.md`): the widget reads the active lists and would otherwise keep offering an archived list's lead until its hourly sweep. Undo of a delete changes nothing in the database, so it asks for nothing.
- While the lists load, the screen shows the shared list skeleton, never a blank body and never "No lists yet" before the lists are known (ADR 0006 as amended; `ListsManagerLoadingPreview`).

**List creation.**
Decided in the owner's review of the prototype on 2026-10-07 (`vision/flows/owner-review-2026-10-07.md`, decisions 13 and 14). Until then a bottom sheet on Lists ("Choose a template" over a two-column grid of six tiles, then "Name your list" and "Create") made the list and opened its List settings; Home's "New list" opened Lists with the sheet up.

- **LIST-28: New list, step by step.** A full-screen flow of its own (`Routes.NewList`, `lists/new`), one decision per step, in the owner's order: Start with (LIST-29), Next; Name, Next; How often, Next; Add people, then "Create list". The app bar shows "New list" and "Step 2 of 4" (3 steps for the list that fills itself, whose How often step is its last and whose button there is "Create list"; "Create list" on both last steps, one word for one action). Home's "New list" and "Create your first list" and the Lists screen's "New list" open it; the older `lists?openCreate=true` still works by opening it over Lists, once.
  - **Name**: one field, "List name", filled from the template's name (empty for "Start from blank"), focused with the keyboard up, the keyboard's action Next. A blank name cannot go on: Next is disabled and the ViewModel refuses it.
  - **How often**: the List settings day wheel (`IntervalDaysPicker`, LIST-30, ADR 0011), set from the template's rhythm (every 2 days, Keep in touch's own, for "Start from blank" and the list that fills itself).
  - **Add people**: the contact picker in Collect mode (`PickerMode.Collect`, `Routes.collectPeople`), which chooses people for a list that does not exist yet: it offers everyone, opens with the people already chosen ticked, and its button ("Add"; TalkBack hears "Add 3 people", no list named) writes nothing but hands the selection back to the flow through the nav entry. The step then shows the People section List settings shows (`MembersPreview`: the count with "Add people", and "Remove {name} from list" per person). With nobody chosen its buttons are "Add people" and, quieter, "Create without people". Onboarding's precedent (make the list first, then add people into it) was not followed, because a list made before Create would be left behind by anyone who stopped part way.
  - **Back** steps back and keeps everything entered; the flow's entries live in its ViewModel's SavedStateHandle, so a process death keeps them too. A template fills in only what the user has not: it renames the list only while the name is blank or still the last template's, and sets How often only until the wheel has been turned. Back on the first step, or the close control on any later one, with something entered (a template picked counts) asks "Discard this list?" with "Keep going" and "Discard"; with nothing entered it just leaves.
  - **Create** makes the list, its rhythm and its people in one transaction (`CreateListUseCase`): the list last in the order (`max(sortOrder) + 1` over every list, archived ones included, LIST-02), Keep in touch with parameters for the chosen interval built by `toKeepInTouchEvery` (the helper List settings' day wheel writes through), and one membership per person, due now, with the due count recomputed in the same write. A list that fills itself takes no people. Nudges start as the sheet's did: on, any time of day, and no stored schedule. A failure part way leaves nothing behind; the flow says "Couldn't save your change" and keeps the user on the step with everything entered. On success it returns to whichever screen opened it, Home or Lists, where the new list is already in place and the snackbar says "Created {name}." (shown only while Orbit is on screen, so never under the privacy curtain). Tested: `NewListViewModelTest`, `NewListContentTest`, `CreateListUseCaseTest`, `OrbitNavHostTest`, `RoutesTest`, `ContactPickerViewModelTest` (Collect).

"Mentors" (every couple of months) is gone: the How often wheel reaches 60 days for anyone who wants that rhythm. Each rhythm tile is tinted a step along one ramp from the theme's accent tint toward its surface (`rhythmTemplateTints`, in the theme layer, the way the Home card's band and the heat ramp are derived), so more often reads warmer in every theme and mode; the warmest stop is stepped toward the surface until the tile's name (fg) and subtitle and icon (fgMuted) clear 4.5:1, and `ThemeContrastTest` checks all three stops in every curated theme, Wallpaper hue and dial hue, both modes. The tint is decoration: each subtitle says the rhythm in words. The tiles are one radio group for TalkBack, each at least 48dp, the chosen one outlined in ink with a check (not the accent: the step's one accent is Next). Tested: `CreateListTemplateCatalogTest`, `TemplateSelectionSemanticsTest`, `ThemeContrastTest`.
- The list picker makes a new list inline when the user has no lists yet (2026-06-09 #26), so a first "Add to lists" has no "create a list first, then come back" detour.
- **LIST-20: One way to create a list on Lists.** The floating "New list" button when lists exist; the centred "New list" button when there are none. The app bar's "+" was removed (2026-10-05): three create controls on one screen read as unfinished (UX rubric D2), and the empty state showed two accent elements.
- **LIST-23: Tapping a list opens its cards, everywhere.** On Lists a row tap opened List settings while the same tap on Home opened the list's deck; one object, two meanings (UX rubric D2). Tested: `ListsManagerViewModelTest` and `ListConfigViewModelTest` (a failing source shows Error, Try again recovers, a missing list is NotFound).
- **LIST-21: List settings spend no accent on settings.** Only the app bar's "Done" is in the accent: the foot-of-form Done is Secondary and the active-hours bar was ink on a muted track (both were accent until 2026-10-06, so a list with active hours showed three accent elements; the bar went with LIST-25). Each setting that has a value reads as one sentence with the value as its argument ("Aim for every 14 days", "Added in the last 30 days", "The top 20% of the people you call"), so a translation can reorder the words (voice.md, "keep a sentence whole"); the wheel's TalkBack value stays its own words.
- Each template makes the rhythm its subtitle names (2026-10-05; before, every template made the same 2-day list): since LIST-28 by starting How often there, and Create writes what How often ends on, for "Start from blank" and the list that fills itself too. **List Configuration (per list).**
Decided in the owner's review of the prototype on 2026-10-07 (`vision/flows/owner-review-2026-10-07.md`, sections 8 to 11):

- **LIST-30: One "How often" control for every list.** Keep in touch, Late night and Energize are one calculation with different numbers, so List settings shows one rhythm control, "How often" (the shared day wheel, `IntervalDaysPicker`, ADR 0011), for every list, smart lists included, and the rhythm choice ("Rhythm": Keep in touch, Late night, Energize) is gone from List settings and from Make your first list. A Late night or Energize list shows its real base interval (`baseIntervalHours`: 72 hours, "Aim for every 3 days"; 24 hours, "Aim for every day"). Turning the wheel makes it an ordinary list at the interval chosen: `ListConfigViewModel.setIntervalHours` writes Keep in touch parameters built by `toKeepInTouchEvery` through `withIntervalHours` (never `cooldownMinHours` alone; the rule engine README's "Interval honesty") and gives the list the Keep in touch template. A Keep in touch list keeps its other numbers; a Late night or Energize one takes Keep in touch's reset percentages and skip penalty (the helper's KDoc lists each). Settling the wheel where it was writes nothing, so a list nobody changes keeps its engine; nothing is migrated and the engines stay. A list whose rhythm cannot be read (no template, or an override that no longer decodes) says "This list has no rhythm yet. Choose how often below to set one." over the wheel, and any choice gives it one. The Lists row describes every regular list by its interval too (a smart list's row names its rule). The wheel takes a number and hands one back, so the step-by-step New list flow and Contact detail's custom schedule draw the same one. One day reads "Aim for every day" and "Every day", not "every 1 day". Tested: `RuleIntervalTest`, `ListConfigViewModelTest`, `ListsManagerViewModelTest`, `IntervalDaysPickerTest`.
- **LIST-25: Time of day.** One single-choice group of chips, "Any time", "Mornings", "Afternoons", "Evenings", "Nights" (`TimeOfDayPicker`, the radio-group precedent of `ImportRangeChipGroup`), replaces the active-hours editor ("Always active" with start and end time pickers). Each part is a window on the existing `activeHoursStart` / `activeHoursEnd` columns, written through the same atomic write: Any time both null, Mornings 7am to noon, Afternoons noon to 5pm, Evenings 5pm to 9pm, Nights 9pm to 7am (across midnight, which the window check already handles). Mornings start at 7 because `NudgeScheduler.effectiveSchedule` adds the window's start as a nudge time when none of the list's times falls inside it. A stored window that is no part (one set with the old pickers) shows as one more selected chip, "Custom: 9am to 5pm", and is never rewritten until the user picks a part. Under the group one line says what the choice does, with the hours in the phone's 12 or 24 hour format ("Nudges for this list come only in the evening, from 5pm to 9pm."). The window gates when the list's nudge may post and never changes its nudge days; a change reschedules the nudge immediately (see `features/notifications/README.md`). "When to nudge" says when the nudge really comes under the time of day, not the times as stored: its line is the schedule the scheduler runs (`NudgeScheduler.effectiveSchedule`, public on its companion for this) less the times the worker's gate holds back (`isInActiveWindow`), through one reading of the two (`nudgePlan`, `NudgeScheduleSection.kt`). So the default 10am under Evenings reads "Every day at 5pm", with "10am is outside this list's time of day, so the nudge comes at 5pm instead." under it, or "... so no nudge comes then." when another chosen time still comes; Make your first list's summary says "Every day at 5pm" too, without the note, since it shows no times. Until 2026-10-08 both read the stored "Every day at 10am" a line below "Nudges for this list come only in the evening". Tested: `TimeOfDayMappingTest`, `ListConfigViewModelTest`, `ListSettingsControlsTest`, `NudgePlanTest`.
- **LIST-26: Rename from the title.** The list's name is the app bar's title and its rename control: the name with a pencil, one button that TalkBack hears as "Rename list, {name}" ("Rename list" under the curtain). Tapping it turns the title into the shared single-line field (`OrbitTextField`, ADR 0012), named "List name" and opening with the cursor after the old name, with "Cancel" and "Save list name" (48dp, labelled) in place of Done and no back arrow, and no Done at the foot of the form while the edit is open: the edit ends only in Save or Cancel. The foot's Done is hidden rather than made to save, because the field saves only through its explicit Save (never on focus loss), and a Done that also saved would be a third way out with a different outcome. Until 2026-10-08 the foot's Done stayed, and tapping it closed the screen and dropped the typed name without a word. "Save list name" and the keyboard's Done save; Cancel and Back leave the edit and keep the name (Back cancels the edit before it leaves the screen). A blank name keeps the old one and an unchanged one writes nothing; a failed save says "Couldn't save your change". Under the privacy curtain the title reads "List" and the field masks its text (PRIV-03). The pane title stays the list's name. The separate "Name" section is gone; Make your first list keeps its own name field. Tested: `ListSettingsControlsTest`, `ListConfigViewModelTest`.
- **LIST-27: Add people at the People header.** The People section's header row, with the count ("11 people"), carries an "Add people" text button with a plus on its right, 48dp tall, so it is in view without scrolling past everyone. The "Add people" row under the last person is gone, so there is one way to do it. Smart lists show neither, and say under the count why there is no remove control: "Orbit fills this list from its rule. To keep someone off it, ignore them." Make your first list uses the same section and has the same button. Tested: `ListSettingsControlsTest`, `MembersPreviewTest`.
- Interval tuning is honest: the day wheel (`IntervalDaysPicker`, shared with New list and Contact detail's custom schedule, ADR 0011) commits whole days through `withIntervalHours`, which moves both cooldown bounds together (`IntervalDaysPickerTest`, `RuleIntervalTest`).
- The "People" section (not "Members preview": it adds and removes people) shows the true count: first 20 rows + "Showing 20 of N" with a "Show all" affordance (`MembersPreview`).
- The rename dialog on Lists and the title's rename field here both mask the name under the privacy curtain (PRIV-03, `RenameListDialogCurtainTest`, `ListSettingsControlsTest`); the dialog did not until 2026-10-06.

**Smart lists (2026-10-05).**
- `SmartListMembershipSync` keeps each non-archived smart list's stored members equal to what its rule matches. A contact who starts matching becomes a member, due now; one who stops matching is removed (for "Recently added, not called", that is the moment you call them). The list's due count is recomputed after each change.
- So smart lists surface wherever static lists do: Home ("Next up", due counts), Card view, Browse and its queue, and nudges. Before, their members existed only inside List settings.
- A smart list with no cadence is given Keep in touch.
- A smart list's People section has no remove control (the sync would put a removed person straight back) and says so under the count: "Orbit fills this list from its rule. To keep someone off it, ignore them." Added 2026-10-08, after a 314-person list read as a list that refused to be edited (`MembersPreviewTest`).
- Convert to static keeps the current members as a snapshot and ends syncing (the list is no longer smart); a list with no cadence gets Keep in touch.
- **SMART-08: "Added" means added to the phone.** The "Recently added, not called" rule ("Added in the last 30 days") dates a person by the earlier of two instants: when Orbit first saw them (`firstSeenByAppAt`) and the phone's own last-updated time for the contact, captured when Orbit first saw it and never moved after (`deviceUpdatedAt`). Both are upper bounds on when the person was saved, so the earlier is the closer one, and it can only make someone look older, never newer: an unreliable device time can drop a person from the list but never add an old contact to it. One function decides this (`contactAddedAt`, `domain/smart/ContactAddedAt.kt`), and the picker's "Recently added" sort reads it too. Until 2026-10-07 the rule read first sight alone; the first contacts sync (and a restore from backup) gives everyone already on the phone the same first-sight instant, so for the length of the window after a fresh install the list held every never-called contact and no setting could narrow it. A phone that reports no last-updated time still falls back to first sight. Pinned by `SmartListEngineTest`.

**Pickers** (`ui/screens/picker/`; page views [Add people](../page-views/picker-contacts.md) and [Add to lists](../page-views/picker-lists.md)).
- The contact picker (BULK-05) files people into a list: search by name or number, sort, filters, an A to Z rail, and a docked bar that commits ("Add"). The list picker (BULK-06) is the reverse: one person, several lists.
- One visual system (2026-10-05, UX rubric D4): every filter is the shared `OrbitFilterChip`, every check mark the shared `OrbitCheckbox` (ink, not accent; the row carries the checkbox semantics), and the sort and "On a list" menus are the shared `OrbitDropdownMenu`. They were Material chips, checkboxes and menus with colour overrides. The only accent on either picker is the commit button; the rail's current letter is bold ink.
- A "Recently added" filter chip, second after "Starred" so it shows without scrolling, narrows to people saved within the window Settings sets ("Recently added: the last 30 days", PICK-07). It dates people by `contactAddedAt` (SMART-08), the definition the "Recently added" sort and the smart rule share. The chip was removed while it read first sight alone, which matched the whole address book or no one; it came back 2026-10-07 (`FilterChipsRowTest`, `ContactPickerViewModelTest`).
- Plain words (rubric D7): "Never called" (sentence case; it was lowercase), "On Inner orbit, Late night" (was "In: ..."), the "Not on a list" filter (was "Unsorted"), "On a list" (was "In list…"), "Select all 14 matches" (was "Select all matching (14)"), "Already added" on a list the person is on (was "added"), "Try removing a filter." - The sort control and both "Clear" actions meet the 48dp floor (they were about 36 and 38dp); the search field has one clear control, not two.
- Privacy curtain: names, photos and list names are masked (list names read "List", or "On 2 lists"), and the list picker's title drops the person's name.

**Picker requirements** (defined 2026-10-05 from what the code already cites for them; PICK-09 is new; PICK-03 defined 2026-10-06, it was cited by the picker's code and no spec):
- **BULK-05: Add people from a list.** The "+" on Browse and "Add people" elsewhere open the contact picker for that list (`pick/contacts?targetListId=...`); the title says "Add people". - **PICK-01: Filter by list.** "On a list" filters the candidates to members of one of your other non-archived lists; the applied filter reads "On {list}".
- **PICK-02: Filters narrow together.** Active filters combine as AND: a contact shows only if it matches every one.
- **PICK-03: Select all N matches.** Offered whenever search or a filter narrows the list and at least one match is unselected ("Select all 14 matches"); capped at 200, above which a note says to narrow the search; never in Re-link, which picks exactly one person.
- **PICK-04: One row per person.** Avatar, name, a call line ("Last called 3 days ago · 4 calls", or "Never called" for no calls, never "0 calls"), the lists they're on (never an archived one, LIST-24), and a check mark; the "Never called" filter reads `callCount == 0` directly. Each row's three dots (or a long-press) offer "Open in Contacts", which hands the person to the phone's own address book, and Ignore, with a line saying they stay in your phone's contacts.
- **PICK-05: Search composes.** Search uses the shared `ContactSearch` matcher (name with accents folded, or phone digits) and narrows whatever the filters left.
- **PICK-06: A docked commit bar, on both pickers.** With anything selected, a bar docked under the list (not floating over it) shows the count, Clear, and the commit button, which says only "Add". TalkBack hears the whole sentence ("Add 3 people to Inner orbit", "Add to 2 lists"; the noun is said, as in every count of people), since a TalkBack user does not see the count and the list at a glance. Until 2026-10-07 the button showed that sentence beside "3 selected", which said the count twice and squeezed the bar (`BatchCounterTest`). Re-link keeps "Re-link {name}": one person, no count to repeat. The bar never hides the last row; the list picker's floated over its last row until 2026-10-06.
- **PICK-08: Ignored people stay out.** Ignored contacts are hidden from the picker unless "Show ignored" is on; shown, they are muted, tagged "Ignored", and offer Unignore instead of selection.
- **PICK-09: A failed read is an error, not a crash.** Either picker shows "Couldn't load your contacts" / "Couldn't load your lists" with "Try again" when a source fails (the contact picker's address-book read included), instead of an uncaught exception in `viewModelScope`. The selection survives the error.

**Cross-list propagation.**
- Calling contact X updates last-call state everywhere X appears — home, card-view, browse, widget — via Flow.
- There is no per-list last-call timestamp. One `CallEntity` per real call; every list derives its view from the same source.

**List ordering.** User-controlled. No inherent priority — lists are peers.

## Technical

### Architecture

- `ListsManagerViewModel` observes `Flow<List<ListEntity>>` from Room; reorder via `sh.calvin.reorderable` in `ListsManagerScreen`.
- `ListConfigViewModel` observes a single list + its rule config Flow.
- `ListPickerViewModel` (under `ui/screens/picker/`) handles add-to-list flows, including inline list creation.
- `SmartListMembershipSync` (`data/feed/`, `@Singleton`) is started once from `OrbitApp.onCreate` and runs on `@ApplicationScope`. It reconciles each smart list's rows against `SmartListEngine.membership(rule)`, so smart lists use the same surfacing path as static ones instead of a second one.
- Cross-list propagation is automatic because call data is stored with `contactId` only (no `listId`); every list's due computation reads the same `CallEntity` rows.

### Data model

- `ListEntity` — id, name, sortOrder, isArchived, type (`STATIC` | `SMART`), `smartRuleJson` (smart lists), `ruleTemplateId`, active hours (nullable start/end), notificationsEnabled, `ruleParamsOverrideJson`.
- `ListMembershipEntity` — many-to-many with `ContactEntity`, plus per-list schedule state (`nextDueAt`, `skipCount`). A smart list's rows are written by `SmartListMembershipSync`, not by the user.
- Rule config as built: `ruleTemplateId` references `RuleTemplateEntity`; per-list param tweaks live in `ruleParamsOverrideJson` (kotlinx-serialization, total parsing). A per-contact override (`ContactEntity.ruleOverrideJson`) still wins over the list's params.

### Permissions / integrations

None directly.

### Known gotchas

- Rule config JSON parsing must be total: every field nullable and defaulted, tolerant of unknown fields. A rule schema change must not brick existing lists.
- Deleting a list must cascade: remove memberships, cancel scheduled list prompts (`features/notifications/README.md`).

### Not in scope (technical)

- Materializing per-list cooldown snapshots in a cache table. Rule-engine recomputes live.
- Per-list database instances. One Room DB for everything.
