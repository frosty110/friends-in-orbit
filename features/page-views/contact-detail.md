# Contact detail

**Route:** `contact/{contactId}`
**Group:** Contact
**Status:** active
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-05
**Index:** [Page views](../PAGE_VIEWS.md)

What a user expects to see or do here:

- See photo, name, number, and which lists they're on
- Read call stats: last called, total calls, average length, longest gap, and "Usually" (the part of the day they are usually called), with "Not enough calls yet" where there isn't enough history
- Scroll recent call history with direction + duration; "View all calls" in the overflow opens Call history for just this person
- Add and read notes (jump straight to the note input from some entry points); edit or delete a note from its "More" menu, as well as by long-press or swipe
- With the privacy curtain on, see no name, photo, list name or note body
- If the person can't be loaded, see why and try again
- Pause the person for a chosen duration; while paused, see "Paused until {date}" (or "Paused until you unpause") under the number and Unpause from the overflow, with undo
- Ignore them, or add them to more lists
- Re-link if they were unlinked from a device contact: pick the phone contact, and it merges into this person (history kept), with undo
