# List settings / configuration

**Route:** `lists/{listId}/config`
**Group:** Lists
**Status:** active
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-05
**Index:** [Page views](../PAGE_VIEWS.md)

What a user expects to see or do here:

- Name the list
- Choose a rhythm (Keep in touch, Late night, Energize) and how often to aim for, in plain words ("Every 14 days"), smart lists too
- Set active hours (or always active); they limit when a nudge may post, never which days it nudges
- Turn nudges on or off and choose when they come
- Preview members, add people, remove with undo
- For smart lists: edit the rule, or make it a regular list (current members stay; a list with no rhythm gets Keep in touch). The confirmation uses the button's words: "Make this a regular list?", "The 5 people here now stay, and the list stops adding people by itself. This can't be undone.", "Make it regular", or "No one is on this list right now, ..." when it matches no one (until 2026-10-05: "Convert to a static list?", "snapshots ... as permanent members", "Convert")
- Nudge days are seven 48dp toggles; on a narrow phone (360dp) they wrap into two rows rather than shrink. Days and times read as one line ("Weekdays at 10am"); "No days selected: nudges off", "No time set: tap “Add time”", and a "Nudges paused" badge while nudges are off (these had em dashes, and the badge said "Muted")
- The time picker and the active-hours bar's ticks follow the phone's 12 or 24 hour setting ("12a 6a 12p 6p" or "00 06 12 18"); both were always 12-hour until 2026-10-05
- Changes save as you go; "Done" is the only accent on the screen (LIST-21)
