# Home

**Route:** `home`
**Group:** Core loop
**Status:** active
**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-05
**Index:** [Page views](../PAGE_VIEWS.md)

What a user expects to see or do here:

- A calm date header, no count of people "due" (HOME-6)
- One full-width card per list: the list's name and size, who is next up with a face and a warm line on how long it has been, and a 7-day rhythm strip
- Tap a card to open that list's deck; tap the quiet phone button on "Next up" to call that person straight away (HOME-9)
- Tap a day on the rhythm strip to see who you talked to that day (HOME-8)
- Long-press a card for Add people, List settings, Pause nudges, Archive, Delete (with undo)
- Create a new list, or "Create your first list" on a fresh install
- Reach Search, Lists and Settings from the app bar
- After a call, a banner offers "Add a note" or "Dismiss"
