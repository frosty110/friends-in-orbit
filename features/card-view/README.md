## Product

### Why it exists

Card view is the core loop. One contact at a time, one yes-or-no decision. The whole mission — reducing cognitive load — lives or dies here. When this screen works, the rest of the app is scaffolding; when it fails, no other feature compensates.

### User story

As a user, I see one person at a time with just enough context to decide whether to call them right now. I press Call, swipe right to surface them sooner, or swipe left to defer.

### Behavior

**Layout.** Photo + contact name dominate above the fold. Then the reason to call now: how long it has been and the pair's usual rhythm, and the last note you wrote. Stats present but secondary. List context shown as a small badge. Later and Sooner buttons, labelled, either side of Call in the thumb zone for users who don't want to swipe.

**Privacy curtain** (2026-10-05). With the curtain on (app backgrounded), the card face shows "Contact" in place of the person's name and derives the avatar initial from it, like the app bar. Before, the face still showed the real name and initials. Convention: `features/privacy-and-lock/README.md`, "Quick-hide on focus loss".

**Smart lists** (2026-10-05). A smart list's rule matches are stored as members, due when they start matching (`SmartListMembershipSync`, see `features/orbit-lists/README.md`), so its card surfaces people like a static list's. Before, it always showed the no-members empty state.

**Context data.** Last call time, call count, average call length, earliest/latest call time-of-day, when you last spoke. Neutral framing — no streaks, no shame.

**Interactions.**
- **Call button** → dials (see Technical → Permissions for dial mechanism). It is the only thing on the screen that dials.
- **Tap the card face** → opens the person's details.
- **Swipe right, or Sooner** → surface sooner (shorter cooldown on this list). The button plays the same fly-off as the swipe (CARD-08).
- **Swipe left, or Later** → defer (longer cooldown on this list). The same (CARD-08).
- **Undo** → each Later or Sooner shows a snackbar naming the person and when they come back, with Undo that restores the prior membership schedule (`nextDueAt` + `skipCount`).
- **Left untouched** → after four seconds, "Later · Thursday" and "Sooner · Tomorrow" fade in at the card's top corners for a moment, a few times, until the swipe is learnt (CARD-09).
- **Log a connection** → beside "Open details", for a conversation Orbit couldn't see ("had dinner yesterday"): the same sheet Contact detail opens, and the deck moves on (CARD-10).
- **After a call** → the deck moves on by itself once the call log shows the call (CORE-04). A call that connected and lasted a minute or more opens the page for writing about it by itself (CARD-11); a shorter one says "Called {name}" with "Add a note", which opens the same page (CARD-03, NOTE-04 in `features/contact-detail/README.md`; until 2026-10-07 it opened the person with the note field focused, NOTE-02).
- **The list menu** ("More actions for {list}") → "Browse people", "Add people", "List settings", in that order. "Add people" is not offered on a smart list, in the menu or on its empty deck: a smart list's members are its rule's matches (`features/orbit-lists/README.md`), and anyone added by hand was removed by the next sync with no word. The smart list's empty deck reads "No one matches this rule right now." with "List settings" instead. Nor is it offered while the list's type is not yet known, on the Loading and Error decks (`CardViewUiState.listType` is null there): until 2026-10-06 the menu asked "is it smart?", so a failed smart list offered Add people.

**Requirements** (2026-10-05, from [`vision/ux-rubric.md`](../../vision/ux-rubric.md)):

- **CARD-01: Only Call dials.** Tapping the card face opens the person's details; the labelled Call button is the only control that places a call. A call reaches another person and cannot be undone, so it is never one stray tap away. (The face used to dial; a call was placed by accident in review.)
- **CARD-02: Later and Sooner are named, and each undo is its own.** The side buttons read "Later" and "Sooner" on screen and to TalkBack, and the card face offers Later, Sooner and Call as accessibility actions. Each action's snackbar names the person and says when they come back ("Sarah will come up again tomorrow."). A newer snackbar replaces an older one at once, and Undo carries a token so only the newest action can be undone. (Snackbars used to queue while the undo slot held only the latest action, so Undo on the first of three quick swipes reverted the third person.)
- **CARD-03: A call is acknowledged once it is real.** Once the call log confirms a call placed from the card, within 15 seconds of returning, the snackbar says "Called {first name}" with "Add a note", which opens the post-call note page for their latest connected call (NOTE-04; their details with the note field focused, NOTE-02, until 2026-10-07). Confirmation is evidence of the call: a connected call for that person logged at or after the dial, or the deck moving past them (the call re-scheduled them behind someone else). It used to be motion alone, and on a one-member list, or when everyone else is further out, the person stays at the head after a real call, so nothing was ever said. An unanswered dial logs an attempt, not a connection, and is never acknowledged. Nothing is said if the call is not confirmed. Any swipe cancels the wait, so a deck that moved for another reason is never credited as a call.
- **CARD-04: Why now, in human terms.** The face shows when you last spoke ("You spoke today.", "You spoke yesterday.", "You spoke 3 weeks ago.", in the app's one "ago" wording from `formatAgo`; until 2026-10-06 it read "3 weeks since you last spoke.", which for the most common gaps rendered the "N days since" framing voice.md never says), the pair's usual rhythm once there are four calls ("You usually talk about every 2 weeks.", the median gap, stated as a fact, never a deadline), and the most recent note from the last 30 days, quoted, ahead of the statistics. The note is hidden under the privacy curtain.
- **CARD-05: "All quiet for now."** When nobody on the list is due, the empty state says so calmly and names who comes up next and when. It never says "caught up" or "done": the queue is continuous by design (HOME-6 in `vision/00-home/00-home.md`, `SurfaceResult.kt`), so nothing should read as a cleared backlog.
- **CARD-06: The card fits the screen it is on.** On a short, wide window (landscape on a phone: wider than tall and under 480dp high) the card sits on the left and its actions (Call, Later, Sooner, Open details) in a column on the right. Stacked, the face got about 120dp of height. Above 130% text the Call button takes its own full-width row with Later and Sooner beneath it (between them it was too narrow for one word). In any orientation the face scrolls when its content outgrows it (large text, a long note), so nothing is ever clipped, and the list chip sits in the face's flow, so a long list name wraps above the avatar instead of over it.
- **CARD-07: A failed read is an error with Try again, not a crash.** When the deck cannot be read (a database error in any of the feed's sources), the screen says "Something's off here." / "Nothing is lost. Try again in a moment." with "Try again" as its one accent and "Go home" beneath, and the list's name stays in the app bar when it is known. Try again rebuilds the subscription (`CardFeed` evicts the failed entry, the BROWSE-06 precedent). A list id that does not parse (a bad deep link) is the same error, but it offers "Go home" only, as the accent: Try again cannot re-read an id that never parsed, and until 2026-10-06 it was offered there anyway and did nothing (rules.md Code 3). The state carries the difference (`Error.canRetry`), so the screen never guesses. With no list name to title the app bar, the deck is announced to TalkBack by its heading. The failure is caught inside `CardFeed` before its process-scoped `stateIn`: an exception escaping an Eagerly started flow on the application scope has no handler and crashed the app, and a `StateFlow` never throws to its collectors, so the ViewModel's own catch could not see it. Before this, the Error state existed in the contract but was unreachable, and a malformed id rendered "All quiet for now." over a list that does not exist.

**Requirements** (2026-10-07, from the owner's review of the prototype, [`vision/flows/owner-review-2026-10-07.md`](../../vision/flows/owner-review-2026-10-07.md) decisions 3, 4 and 5):

- **CARD-08: Later and Sooner play the swipe.** Tapping Later or Sooner flies the card off in that direction exactly as a swipe does, then moves on: the same settle (`CardSwipeFrameState.requestSwipeLeft` / `requestSwipeRight` drive the frame's own `animateTo`), the same haptic, the same single commit, so the buttons show that the card can be swiped. TalkBack's Later and Sooner actions take the same path. With Android's animations turned off (`LocalReducedMotion`) every settle is a snap: the move lands in a frame, with its haptic and one commit, and nothing flies (rules.md Design 8). This was already true when the owner asked for it (the prototype did not show it): on a device the system's animator scale made the spring instant, and the buttons had driven the frame's settle since the repository's first commit (2026-06-17). On 2026-10-07 the frame started reading the setting itself, so the snap is in code and a test holds both halves (`CardViewMovesTest`).
- **CARD-09: Idle hints teach the swipe.** When the card has sat untouched for 4 seconds (no drag, no button, no tap), two quiet hints fade in at the card's top corners: an arrow pointing left with "Later · Thursday" and "Sooner · Tomorrow" with an arrow pointing right, each saying when that person would come up after that move. They hold about 2.5 seconds, fade out, and come back every 12 seconds while nothing happens, at most 3 times for each person shown (kept across rotation). Any touch hides them at once, with no fade, and restarts the 4 seconds. They never run while the screen is paused or a sheet covers the card. Once the user has made 5 Later or Sooner moves in total, by swipe, button or TalkBack, they stop for good (`AppPrefs.cardMovesMade`, which a new process keeps). With animations off they appear and go without fading. The "when" is the move's own: `SkipContactUseCase.preview` and `SurfaceSoonerUseCase.preview` run the function each move writes with, asked fresh before each appearance, and the words come from the same `comesUp` bucket as the move's snackbar, worded to stand alone ("Thursday", "In 2 weeks", "Tomorrow", "Today"), so a hint and the snackbar after it name the same day; where a move cannot be worked out, the hint says "Later" or "Sooner" alone. Decoration for sighted touch users: cleared from what TalkBack is given (the labelled buttons and the face's actions already teach it), no names (nothing for the curtain to mask), and no accent: the Call button is the card's one (rules.md Design 5). The pills sit in the band of the face that never carries the name or why now (the list chip and the avatar), and stack when the pair does not fit on one line, so no word breaks. An exception to rules.md Design 8's "no motion on idle surfaces" (CORE-09, and the June vision's `CARD-2`, "a card that stirs when you stall reads as anxious"), decided by the owner on 2026-10-07 and bounded so it stays calm: the card itself never moves, only two labels change opacity, nothing loops forever, and the hints end once the swipe is learnt.
- **CARD-10: Log a connection from the card.** "Log a connection", beside "Open details" under the card in the same quiet style (48dp), opens the sheet Contact detail uses (`ui/components/LogConnectionSheet`: "We connected" or "Couldn't reach them", Today, Yesterday or a picked date, an optional note) for the person the card showed when it was tapped, even if the deck moves while the sheet is open. Saving writes through `LogConnectionUseCase`, the one write Contact detail calls too: a MANUAL connection or an ATTEMPT through MarkCalledUseCase's atomic reschedule, with the note back-dated to it. The person goes back into the rhythm and the deck moves on through its feed, as after a call. Then "Logged. Kai comes up again in 2 weeks." for a connection, "Attempt logged. Kai comes up again on Sunday." for an attempt (Contact detail's "Logged." and "Attempt logged." with the card's "comes up" words), "Logged." alone when there is no future time to name, and "They" in place of the name while the privacy curtain is down. A write that fails says "Couldn't save your change". A log cancels a pending "Called {name}" (CARD-03): a deck that moved because of a log is not evidence of a call.
- **CARD-11: The note page after a call worth a note.** When a call placed from the card is confirmed (CARD-03's evidence) and the call log has it as connected and a minute or more long (`WaitingCalls.MIN_SECONDS`, NOTE-05's floor, the one constant Home's stack and the notification after a call use), the page for writing about that call (NOTE-04) opens by itself, in place of the "Called {name}" snackbar, naming the call (`Routes.postCallNote(contactId, callEventId)`). A shorter call keeps the snackbar, whose "Add a note" opens the same page; an attempt never opens it. Leaving the page lands on the deck, which has moved on to the next person. It opens at most once for a call: the pending dial is consumed on the first resume after it, so a second resume, a rotation or a restored process cannot act on it again; and it is kept in the ViewModel's `SavedStateHandle` (an id and a time, no name), so a process Android killed during a long call still opens the page on return. The notification after a call (NOTIF-16) is posted only while Orbit is in the background; on the dial-and-return path both background routes to the sync wait at least the 10 second debounce, and returning to the card replaces that pending sync with one run in the foreground, so nothing is posted unless the user stayed away about 10 seconds or more after hanging up (a phone that shows its own call log first). For that case the card withdraws the person's notification when it opens the page. And if the user taps that notification instead, the tap brings Orbit back to the card (onNewIntent, then onResume), the resume makes the card open the page, and the tap's route opens it too, in the same moment and in either order; until 2026-10-08 that stacked two pages, because the two routes spell the person differently ("c-7" and "7") and the deep-link guard compares strings. The nav host now opens the note page through one owner keyed on the call event id (`PostCallNotePages` in `nav/OrbitNavHost.kt`, NOTE-04): whichever comes second opens nothing, and once a call's page has opened by any way in, the card never opens it by itself again, so a dial still pending when the card resumes after the user saved their note from the notification's page (or said "Not now") asks nothing. That is why the card's own open does not check the back stack.

**Physics.** The card moves with the finger from touch slop and tilts as dragged, up to about 8 degrees; the "Later" and "Sooner" hint chips fade in at the destination edge from the first few dp of travel. Commit and snap-back settle on one spring (`OrbitMotion.springCardCommit`: low bounce, medium-low stiffness, overshoot held under 5%), driven by `anchoredDraggable`, so a re-grab mid-settle is a new drag, not a fight with a tween; with animations off the spring is a snap (CARD-08). Haptic buzz on swipe commit, not at threshold entry.

**Cross-list propagation.** Calling from this screen updates last-call state on every list this contact belongs to — immediately, via Flow.

**Empty states.** Teaching empty states as built, each the shared `OrbitScreenMessage` with the next step as its one action and "Go home" beneath: `EmptyNoMembers` (the list has no members: "Add people", or "List settings" on a smart list) and `EmptyNothingEligible` ("All quiet for now.", CARD-05, naming who comes back soonest and when; a paused person for when the pause lifts, and nobody for a pause until you unpause). `Error` is CARD-07. The app bar carries the list's name in every state that knows it ("List" under the curtain; blank while loading and when the list itself could not be read), so TalkBack announces the pane and "this list" is named on screen. The loop stays continuous: there is no terminal state, only a kind word while nobody is due. Voice per `features/_foundations/voice.md`.

## Technical

### Architecture

- `CardSwipeFrame` owns the drag state (replaced the generic `SwipeableCard` component, deleted 2026-06-09); drag state stays local so only the card recomposes during drag.
- `CardViewViewModel` exposes `StateFlow<CardViewUiState>` (`Loading` / `Ready` / `EmptyNoMembers` / `EmptyNothingEligible` / `Error`), a thin subscriber to the singleton `CardFeed` through `retryCount.flatMapLatest`, so Try again re-subscribes (CARD-07). `CardFeed`'s placeholder snapshot is `loaded = false` and maps to `Loading`, so a cold open never flashes an empty deck; a failed read arrives as a snapshot with `error` set, caught inside the feed before its `stateIn`.
- The surfaced contact is hydrated via `withCallStats` (last called / avg length / total calls) + `withCallPatterns` (hour histogram for the heat strip) from the snapshot's recent calls.
- On swipe commit: VM captures the prior membership schedule, runs the mutation (`SkipContactUseCase` / `SurfaceSoonerUseCase`), then offers Undo that restores via `UndoStack` + `ListRepository.restoreMembershipSchedule`.
- Post-dial follow-through: `onCall` remembers the dialed contact and the instant (in the `SavedStateHandle` since 2026-10-07, so a process death during the call keeps them; the first name stays in memory); on resume the VM consumes them, triggers an immediate call-log sync and waits up to 15 seconds for evidence of the call before acknowledging it (CARD-03): a `Ready` for the same person whose `lastCallAt` (connections only, never an attempt) is at or after the dial, or the deck moving past them. Then it reads the person's newest call-log call at or after the dial: one that lasted `WaitingCalls.MIN_SECONDS` or more opens the note page (CARD-11), anything else is the "Called {name}" snackbar. There is no "did you talk?" confirmation: the call log is the source of truth, and off-log connections use "Log a connection", on the card (CARD-10) or the contact screen.
- One-off messages are a `CardMessage` (`Undoable` with a token, `Called`, `Logged` with its nameless twin, `OpenNote`, `Failed`); the screen collects them with `collectLatest`, so the newest replaces the one on screen. `OpenNote` is the one that is not a snackbar: the screen navigates to the note page, which withdraws the person's notification after a call as it opens (NOTIF-16), whichever way it is reached. The navigation goes through the nav host's `PostCallNotePages`, which opens one page per call (see CARD-11).
- CARD-09's hints: `CardSwipeHints.kt` holds the clock (`SwipeHintsState`, one per person, saved across rotation), the touch watcher (a pointer modifier on the initial pass that consumes nothing) and the overlay, drawn in `CardSwipeFrame`'s ghost slot so it never moves with the card. What they say comes from `CardViewViewModel.moveHints`, a suspend query the clock asks just before each appearance rather than a field on `CardViewUiState`: a "when" fixed at emission would go stale while the card sits, and a state change while a swiped card is held off-screen would make the frame bring the old card back. The move count is `AppPrefs.cardMovesMade`, written on every Later and Sooner in its own coroutine (a failure there costs only a few more hints, so it is not reported to the user: the count is not their data).
- CARD-10's sheet open state is screen state with one owner, in `CardViewContent` above the deck's branches (rules.md Code 7), holding the person it was opened for.

### Data model

- Reads: `ContactEntity`, `CallEventEntity` (stats computed in Kotlin — no `CallStatEntity`), `ListMembershipEntity`, `NoteEntity`.
- Writes: swipe outcomes update `ListMembershipEntity.nextDueAt` + `skipCount` (no dedicated `CooldownEntity`); "Log a connection" (CARD-10) writes `CallEventEntity(source = MANUAL)` or `(source = ATTEMPT)` through `LogConnectionUseCase`, and a `NoteEntity` back-dated to it when a note is given. Preferences: `card_moves_made` (CARD-09), a count only.

### Permissions / integrations

- **Haptics:** `VIBRATOR_SERVICE` — no manifest permission needed.
- **Dial:** default `ACTION_DIAL` (no permission required). `CALL_PHONE` → `ACTION_CALL` upgrade is an open product question above.

### Known gotchas

- Card view must not be nested inside any `LazyColumn` or scrollable parent — gesture will fight with parent scroll.
- Recomposition during drag is expensive; keep drag state isolated to the card composable, not hoisted to `HomeState` or similar parent.
- Drag and settle are one `AnchoredDraggableState` (`CardSwipeFrame`), so a re-grab during the settle is a new drag; `animate*AsState` could not be cancelled that way and was replaced.

### Not in scope (technical)

- Persisting drag position across process death. Cards restart clean — product-acceptable.
- Prefetching next contact's photo. Coil's cache handles this.
